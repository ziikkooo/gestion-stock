How stock movements work

Entry: adds received items.

Exit: removes dispatched items. Stock cannot become negative.

Adjustment: replaces the recorded balance with the actual counted quantity. Include a reason.

Every successful operation records the administrator, date and time (UTC), previous balance, signed change, resulting balance and reason.

If the balance changed while you were counting, an adjustment is rejected; reload and review the stock.
